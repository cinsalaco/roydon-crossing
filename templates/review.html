Observations vs predictions

Measured at the crossing (from RTT's recorded train times)

These are the numbers that calibrate the model: seconds from lights-on to the train actually reaching Roydon, and from the train to the barrier lifting. They use the actual times RTT records after each train has passed, fetched nightly.

How far out was the app at the time?

Positive means the real event happened later than the app predicted; negative means earlier. "Lights started" and "Barrier up" are the two that calibrate the lead and trail times. Split by direction and stop/pass to see whether one number fits all trains.
